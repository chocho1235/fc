How to read it

  • Bookie is the current decimal price from the fixture feed.
  • Fair is the model's break-even price.
  • Bet from adds the value margin, so lower odds are a pass.
  • Key out means higher-impact unavailable players.
  • Total out is wider squad availability and can include lower-impact players.
  • Availability not loaded means we do not have injury data for that match.
  • Importance runs low to high; high usually means title, Europe or relegation pressure.
  • Over 2.5 is now a separate calibrated model, but the value-bet sample is still small.
