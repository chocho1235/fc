How it works

  • 80%+ is roughly odds of 1.25 or shorter.
  • One pick per fixture — always the highest-probability outcome, whether result or builder leg.
  • The smart stack multiplies probabilities — each extra leg reduces combined confidence, so fewer legs often beats more.
  • Hit rates update every time the model retrains on settled results.
  • Never chase a losing stack — each is independent.
